## What she does

- Creates **content for small businesses**: photo and video from half-day shoots.
- Does **not** do social media management, and doesn't want to. Clients get the content and decide
  what to do with it.
- Acts as a **one-stop shop for creative**: she brings everyone to the table. Strategy, the venue,
  models, and a network of videographers, web designers and graphic designers.
- Can do the work herself, bring in her network, or work with the client's own people.
- Positions herself as the client's **creative partner**, not just a content creator ("I'll get in
  the ring with you"). Content creators are a dime a dozen; the partnership is the difference, and
  she says it has been working well.
- **Clients stay in control.** She doesn't dictate the message; she shapes it with them.

## How it feels to work with her

- **The brand is her**: her personality and how comfortable she makes people feel.
- She takes something that feels scary, that clients don't want to do, and makes it **fun,
  comfortable and easy**.
- Instead of content creation that feels cold and plays on people's insecurities, she **boosts their
  confidence** and makes them feel understood.
- Her Google reviews say exactly this, in clients' own words (see Google Business Profile below).

## How she works and prices

- **Half-day shoot = 4 hours, $400** ($100/hr) **plus the completed content**, delivered raw or
  edited. The client decides what to do with it.
- **Once a month per client**: they pay, she makes it easy, shoots the content, takes care of
  everything else, and repeats the next month.
- **Editing into finished cuts ready to post is extra**: as little as $100 up to several hundred,
  discussed, agreed and approved before she does it.
- **Paid clients only.** She does not want to give a lot away for free.
